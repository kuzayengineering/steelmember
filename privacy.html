Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Steelmember is a free reference tool published by Kuzay Engineering for looking up structural steel shape properties. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it — on the web or as the Android app.

Short version

Steelmember does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app does locally

All lookups, searches, and filtering happen entirely on your device — the steel shape data is built into the app itself, and no search terms or usage activity are ever sent anywhere. The app uses your browser's local caching (a service worker) purely so pages you've already visited load faster and work offline; this cached data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

If you use the "Copy" buttons, data is copied to your device's clipboard using your browser's or operating system's built-in clipboard feature — nothing is sent to a server.

Third-party services

The app loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading these fonts involves your device making a request to Google's servers, which may receive standard web request information (such as your IP address) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not share any additional information with Google beyond what your browser sends automatically to load the fonts.

Data we collect

None. We do not operate a backend server for this app, and we do not receive, log, or store any information about how you use it.

Children's privacy

Steelmember is a general-purpose engineering reference tool not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to barrykuzay@gmail.com.

This tool provides experimental data as-is, with no guarantee of accuracy or completeness. Always verify values against current manufacturer or AISC references before relying on them for design work.
